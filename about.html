The Concept

Each day a new original painting is created of a specific, researched UK coastal location. The style is a unique fusion of three randomly chosen famous artists – never a direct copy of any existing work.

Every piece carries the artist’s minute signature “N.Bruce” in the bottom right corner and is offered as an A4 framed print.

How it works

Locations are real places around the British coast (harbours, beaches, cliffs, coves). Artists are selected at random each day and their techniques blended into a single original composition.

To purchase a framed print, use the enquire button on each artwork or contact us directly. New works appear daily.
